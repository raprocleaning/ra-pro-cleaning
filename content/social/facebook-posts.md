## How to get the most out of these

1. **Post the link in the first comment, not the caption.** Facebook shows
   link-free posts to noticeably more people. Caption = story + phone number,
   comment = website.
2. **Video beats photo, before/after beats both.** A 10–20 second clean-with-me
   clip with the BEFORE in the first frame is your best-performing format.
3. **One ask per post.** "Message us" OR "call us" — not four different options.
4. **Reply to every comment within the hour.** Facebook rewards it, and half of
   those commenters are leads.
5. **Rotate the angle:** offer → proof → personality → service spotlight. Posting
   the same discount every time trains people to wait for a discount.
6. **Best times for Denver home services:** weekdays 7–9am and 7–9pm, plus
   Sunday evening when people are dreading the week's chores.
